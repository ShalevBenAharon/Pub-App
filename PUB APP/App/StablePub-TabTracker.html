Backup & Restore

Everything (members, menu, tabs) is saved automatically on this computer — you don't need to do anything for day-to-day use. This is just a safety copy in case this computer is ever replaced, lost, or its browser data is cleared.

Where to put the backup file: when you click Download, your browser saves it to its Downloads folder. Move (or save) that file into your OneDrive or Google Drive folder so it's automatically copied off this computer too. You'll get a reminder here (and on the Log Drinks tab) if it's been a while since your last backup.
